CoPilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 June 2026

CoPilot (the “app”) is a flight-operations companion app for commercial airline pilots. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, and your choices. The app is operated by Adel Attef (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at any time at copilotpilotapp@gmail.com.

The guiding principle of CoPilot is data minimisation: the information the app needs to do its job stays on your device wherever possible, and we only store on our servers the small amount of data required for the optional roster-sharing feature.

1. Your crew-system (eCrew / AIMS) login stays on your device

To show your schedule and logbook, the app logs in to your airline’s crew system (eCrew / AIMS) on your behalf. Your crew-system username and password are stored only on your device, in the device’s protected local storage. They are sent directly from your device to your airline’s servers over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to log you in. They are never transmitted to, processed by, or stored on our servers, and we never have access to them.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following is kept locally on your device and is not uploaded to us:

  • Your roster / schedule and flight details retrieved from the crew system.
  • Your logbook entries and any PDF you export (export is initiated by you and shared only where you choose).
  • Your pilot profile: airline, position, fleet, home base, and the year you joined.
  • Operational tool inputs and settings (e.g. De-Ice, GRF, FDTL, Fuel, Wake-Up alarm times).

If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this on-device information is removed.

3. Data we store for the optional “Friends” feature

CoPilot includes an optional feature that lets you share your roster with pilot colleagues you have added and who have accepted you. This feature is the only part of the app that stores data on our backend, which is provided by Supabase (see “Service providers” below). If you never create a Friends account, no data of yours is stored on our servers.

If you do use Friends, we store:

  • Your account email address and password (the password is handled and securely hashed by Supabase Auth — we never see it in plain text).
  • Your chosen display name and a generated friend code.
  • Your friend relationships (who you have connected with).
  • Roster summaries you choose to share: your duty days for a month, and per-day details such as report/debrief times, scheduled flight legs, and layover hotel — so accepted friends can see your availability.

Only friends you have accepted can view your shared roster. Access is enforced at the database level so that no other user can read your data.

We deliberately do not share or store on our servers:

  • Your crew-system credentials (see section 1).
  • Actual (flown) times, personal memos, or crew lists.
  • Your logbook.

4. Emails we send

If you use the Friends feature, we send transactional emails — for example, a password-reset code when you request one. These are sent from copilotpilotapp@gmail.com. We do not send marketing emails.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not use advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect your device location.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

6. Service providers

We use Supabase (supabase.com) to host the database and authentication for the Friends feature. Supabase processes the Friends data described in section 3 on our behalf. We use Google Play to distribute the app. Transactional email is delivered via Google’s mail servers. These providers process data only as needed to provide their service.

7. Data retention and deletion

On-device data exists only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Friends-feature data is retained until you delete your account. To delete your account and all associated server-side data, email copilotpilotapp@gmail.com from the address on your account, and we will remove it. Removing a friend deletes that sharing relationship for both sides.

8. Security

  • Crew-system credentials never leave your device and are never sent to us.
  • All network connections use encryption (HTTPS / TLS).
  • Friends data is protected by per-user database access rules so you can only read your own data and data shared with you by accepted friends.

9. Children

CoPilot is a professional tool intended for licensed commercial pilots and is not directed to children. It is intended for users aged 18 and over.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email copilotpilotapp@gmail.com.

CoPilot is an independent app and is not endorsed by or affiliated with any airline. Airline and crew-system names are used only to describe interoperability.
