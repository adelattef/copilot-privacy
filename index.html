CoPilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

CoPilot (the “app”) is operated by Adel Attef (“we”, “us”). This policy explains the data the app handles and your choices. Contact us at copilotpilotapp@gmail.com.

1. Data kept on your device

The app can connect to your airline crew system (eCrew / AIMS) using credentials you enter; those credentials are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Your roster, flight details, logbook, exported files, pilot profile, operational tool inputs, settings, notification preferences, scheduled alerts, and background-refresh records are also kept on your device unless shared as described below. With permission, the app may check for roster changes in the background and issue local notifications.

2. Account data

When you create or use an account, Supabase handles:

  • Your email address and password. Supabase handles the password as our authentication provider; we do not receive it directly.
  • Your display name, generated friend code, account identifier, friend and sponsored-access relationships, and recorded agreement version and date.

CoPilot and Crew Board use the same account.

3. Roster sharing

When you refresh a connected roster while signed in, the app uploads roster summaries needed for sharing: duty dates and codes, report and debrief times, scheduled flight legs and times, and layover hotel details. You choose who can view this information by accepting or removing friends; only accepted friends can view your shared roster.

4. Purchases and emails

RevenueCat receives your account identifier and purchase or entitlement information to provide and verify paid access. Apple or Google handles app-store distribution and payment information. Supabase and our transactional email service use your email address only for account verification, password resets, and other account-related messages; we do not send marketing email.

5. How data is used and shared

We use data only to operate accounts, connect friends, share rosters, manage access and purchases, provide support, and keep the app working. Supabase, RevenueCat, Apple, Google, and the transactional email provider receive only the data needed for their services and must protect it consistently with this policy.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not use ads, advertising identifiers, analytics, or tracking.
  • We do not collect device location.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.

7. Retention and deletion

Account, profile, relationship, and agreement data is kept until the account is deleted. Shared roster data is kept in a rolling sharing window and replaced as the roster is updated. Purchase and transaction records may be retained by RevenueCat, Apple, or Google as needed to provide subscriptions and meet legal or accounting requirements.

To request deletion, email copilotpilotapp@gmail.com from the address registered to your account. We will delete the account and associated account, profile, relationship, and shared-roster data we control promptly, and confirm by reply. Because the account is shared with Crew Board, deleting it removes the account from both apps. Messages you sent us may remain in our mailbox.

Data kept only on your device is not removed by an account-deletion request. Remove it using the app or device controls, or by uninstalling the app, subject to your operating system’s behavior.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal data.

9. Children

CoPilot is intended for professional pilots aged 18 or over and is not directed to children.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will update the date above when a revised policy is published. Questions or deletion requests may be sent to copilotpilotapp@gmail.com.
