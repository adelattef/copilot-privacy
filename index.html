CoPilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

CoPilot (the “app”) is published by AAB@safespace (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have. Contact: copilotpilotapp@gmail.com.

1. Information on your device

Most of what the app does happens on your device. Information you enter or keep in the app — your profile, schedule, logbook, tool inputs, settings and exported files — stays on your device, except as described in sections 3 and 4 below. We do not receive it.

2. Account information

When you create an account, our authentication provider handles your email address and password; we never see your password. We also keep your display name, a generated friend code, an account identifier, the connections you make with other users, and the date you accepted the in-app agreement. The same account is used by our companion app Crew Board.

3. Sharing with friends

If you choose to share your schedule with friends, the schedule details you share (dates, duties, times and layover details) are stored on our service so that the friends you have accepted can view them. Only accepted friends can see them. Removing a friend stops sharing with that person.

4. Swap Board

Your verified position. When you open a duty’s details, the app reads your own entry on that duty’s crew list from your airline’s crew system. If it matches the rank and fleet in your profile, the app saves your airline, rank, fleet and staff number to our service. This record lets you use the Swap Board and is saved whether or not you use it. Only you can see it, and it is removed when you delete your account.

Posts. If you post a duty-swap offer, we store it and show it to signed-in pilots whose verified airline, rank and fleet match yours. A post contains:

  • the duty details from your roster for the days you choose — flights, times and layover station (never the hotel’s name);
  • your staff number, rank and fleet;
  • your note and phone number, if you add them.

Everyone who can see your post can see all of this. People who are not signed in cannot see posts. To prevent misuse, we also count how many posts each account makes in a day.

Removing posts. You can remove a post at any time. An expired offer is hidden from everyone at once; it is removed from our service when you next post, and at the latest when you delete your account.

Contacting another pilot. If you tap a pilot’s WhatsApp number, the app opens WhatsApp with a draft message containing your own duties for those dates, your rank, fleet and staff number. You can edit it before sending. The message is sent by WhatsApp under its own terms and does not pass through our service. Sharing your own post from My Posts works the same way, through the app you choose.

5. Purchases

Apple or Google handles payment. A subscription-management service receives your account identifier and purchase status so that paid features can be verified.

6. How information is used and shared

We use information only to operate accounts, connect friends, share schedules, run the Swap Board, manage purchases, answer support requests and keep the app working. Our authentication, subscription-management and email-delivery providers, Apple and Google receive only what their service needs and must protect it in line with this policy. We send account emails only (verification, password reset); no marketing.

7. What we do not do

  • No advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking.
  • No location collection.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.

8. Retention and deletion

Account information is kept until you delete your account. Shared schedule details are replaced as you update them and removed when you stop sharing or delete the account. Swap Board records are removed as described in section 4; the daily post count is kept until you delete your account. Purchase records may be kept by Apple, Google or the subscription-management service as their rules require.

To delete your account, open Profile in the app and choose Delete account, or email copilotpilotapp@gmail.com from your registered address — see the account deletion page. Deletion removes the account from both CoPilot and Crew Board, including your Swap Board records. Information kept only on your device is removed by uninstalling the app.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information.

10. Children

CoPilot is intended for professional pilots aged 18 or over.
