Delete your CoPilot account

App: CoPilot · Developer: Adel Attef · Last updated: 23 June 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your CoPilot account (the optional Friends account) and all data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. From the email address on your CoPilot account, send an email to copilotpilotapp@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my account.
  3. We confirm the request comes from your account email and delete your account. You'll receive a confirmation when it's done.

What gets deleted

All server-side data tied to your account is permanently deleted:

  • Your account (email address and password).
  • Your display name and friend code.
  • Your friend connections.
  • Any roster summaries you had shared with friends.

What is kept

  • Nothing on our servers — deletion removes all of the above.
  • Your crew-system login and on-device data (logbook, schedule, tool settings) are stored only on your device, never on our servers, and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Timing

Account deletion is completed within 30 days of the request, and usually much sooner. No data is retained after deletion.

Questions

Email copilotpilotapp@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
