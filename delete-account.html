Delete your CoPilot account

App: CoPilot · Publisher: AAB@safespace · Last updated: 23 August 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your CoPilot account and all data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. From the email address on your CoPilot account, send an email to copilotpilotapp@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my account.
  3. We confirm the request comes from your account email and delete your account. You'll receive a confirmation when it's done.

What gets deleted

All server-side data tied to your account is permanently deleted:

  • Your account (email address and password).
  • Your display name, friend code and agreement record.
  • Your connections with other users.
  • Any schedule details you had shared with friends.

Good to know

  • One account serves both CoPilot and Crew Board. Deleting it removes your account from both apps.
  • Information kept only on your device (logbook, schedule, tool settings) is not on our servers — remove it by uninstalling the app.
  • Purchase records may be kept by Apple, Google or the subscription-management service as their rules require.
  • No data is retained after deletion, except emails you have sent us, which remain in our mailbox.

Timing

Account deletion is completed promptly after the request is verified, and we confirm by reply.

Questions

Email copilotpilotapp@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
